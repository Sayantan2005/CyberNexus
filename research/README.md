# Community Research

Target community:
Cybersecurity students, beginner ethical hackers and CTF enthusiasts.

## Research questions

1. Where do members currently get cybersecurity news?
2. How do they discover CTFs?
3. Which cybersecurity areas interest them?
4. What problems do they face with fragmented information?
5. Would they use a centralized community platform?
6. Which community features would they use?

## Evidence to collect

- Google Form responses
- 5-10 short interviews
- Screenshots/notes of existing community workflows
- Charts
- User personas
- Feature requirements derived from findings

## Important

Do not fabricate survey results. Collect real responses and report the actual findings.
